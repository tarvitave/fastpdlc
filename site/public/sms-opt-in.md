# SMS opt-in

Last updated 23 August 2026

> We do not currently send SMS messages. This page sets out the terms that will apply
> if and when we do, so that consent is never collected without them being available first.

## What you are consenting to

If you provide your mobile number and tick the SMS consent box, you agree to receive text
messages from FastPDLC about product updates, release announcements, and service notifications
relevant to your account.

Consent to receive SMS is **never a condition of purchase** and is never required
to use FastPDLC, download the software, or subscribe to the email newsletter.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies. We expect no more than **four messages per month**.
Service notifications (for example, a security advisory affecting a release you use) may be sent
outside that cadence.

## Cost

**Message and data rates may apply.** These are set by your mobile carrier, not by
us. Check your plan if you are unsure.

## How to stop

Reply **STOP** to any message to unsubscribe immediately. You will receive one
final confirmation and then nothing further. You can also
[contact us](/contact.html) to be removed.

Reply **HELP** to any message for assistance, or reach us through the
[contact page](/contact.html).

## Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Eligibility

You must be at least 18 and the account holder, or have the account holder's permission, for
the mobile number you provide.

## Your data

Your mobile number is used solely to deliver the messages described above. It is not sold,
rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never
shared with anyone for marketing purposes. See our [privacy policy](/privacy.html) for
how we handle personal data and how to request deletion.

## Changes

We may update these terms; the date above reflects the current version. Material changes
affecting active subscribers will be notified by message before taking effect.
